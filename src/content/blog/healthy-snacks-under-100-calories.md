## Introduction

Smart snacking can make or break a weight loss plan. The right snack keeps you satisfied between meals, prevents overeating at dinner, and delivers nutrients your body needs. The wrong snack — a candy bar, a handful of chips, a sugary coffee drink — can add 300-500 calories without filling you up at all.

Every snack on this list comes in under 100 calories while providing meaningful nutrition through protein, fiber, or both. These are practical, everyday options you can prepare in minutes or grab on the go.

## Fruit-Based Snacks

### 1. One Medium [Apple](/apple) — 95 calories
High in fiber (4.4g) and incredibly portable. The act of chewing an apple slows eating speed and promotes satiety. Pair it with cinnamon for extra flavor.

### 2. One Cup of [Strawberries](/strawberries) — 49 calories
Sweet, satisfying, and loaded with vitamin C. You can eat an enormous portion for under 100 calories.

### 3. One Cup of [Watermelon](/watermelon) — 46 calories
Over 90% water, making it one of the most hydrating and filling snacks available. Perfect for hot days.

### 4. One Cup of [Blueberries](/blueberries) — 85 calories
Rich in antioxidants and fiber. Blueberries are easy to portion and eat without any preparation.

### 5. Two Clementines — 70 calories
Pre-portioned by nature. Clementines are easy to peel, sweet, and perfect for lunchboxes.

### 6. One Medium [Banana](/banana) (small) — 90 calories
A small banana provides quick energy plus potassium. Ideal as a pre-workout snack.

### 7. Frozen Grapes (1 cup) — 62 calories
Freezing grapes turns them into a sweet, crunchy treat that takes longer to eat, naturally controlling portion size.

## Vegetable-Based Snacks

### 8. [Carrots](/carrots) with Mustard (150g carrots) — 65 calories
Crunchy, sweet, and satisfying. The mustard adds tang for essentially zero extra calories.

### 9. [Cucumber](/cucumber) Slices with Everything Bagel Seasoning — 20 calories
A trending snack for good reason. The seasoning provides intense flavor for a negligible number of calories.

### 10. [Celery](/celery) Sticks (200g) — 32 calories
Fill an entire snack bag with celery and you still have room for a dip. Add a tablespoon of [hummus](/hummus) and the total reaches about 57 calories.

### 11. Cherry [Tomatoes](/tomatoes) (200g) — 36 calories
Pop them like candy. Cherry tomatoes are sweet, juicy, and naturally portioned for snacking.

### 12. Roasted [Broccoli](/broccoli) Bites (100g) — 55 calories
Toss broccoli florets with cooking spray and a pinch of salt, then roast at high heat until crispy. A surprisingly addictive snack.

### 13. [Bell Pepper](/bell-peppers) Strips with Salsa (1 pepper + 2 tbsp salsa) — 55 calories
Colorful, crunchy, and the salsa adds flavor for very few calories.

### 14. Steamed Edamame (80g shelled) — 97 calories
A protein-rich vegetable snack providing 9g of protein. Sprinkle with sea salt or chili flakes.

## Protein-Rich Snacks

### 15. One Hard-Boiled [Egg](/eggs) — 78 calories
The most convenient high-protein snack. Provides 6g of protein and keeps you full far longer than any 78-calorie carb snack would. Cook a batch at the start of each week.

### 16. Nonfat Greek Yogurt (100g) — 59 calories
Delivers 10g of protein for under 60 calories. Add a few [strawberries](/strawberries) and you are still well under 100 calories.

### 17. Low-Fat Cottage Cheese (100g) — 72 calories
Slow-digesting casein protein keeps hunger at bay. Top with a sprinkle of pepper or a few cherry [tomatoes](/tomatoes).

### 18. [Turkey](/turkey-breast) Roll-Ups (60g) — 62 calories
Roll slices of turkey breast around [cucumber](/cucumber) sticks or [bell pepper](/bell-peppers) strips for a high-protein, zero-carb snack providing 14g of protein.

### 19. Beef Jerky (25g) — 83 calories
A portable protein powerhouse with about 8g of protein per 25g serving. Choose brands with minimal added sugar.

### 20. [Shrimp](/shrimp) Cocktail (5 large shrimp) — 55 calories
Cold shrimp with a tablespoon of cocktail sauce is a surprisingly low-calorie snack that delivers 10g of protein.

## Nuts, Seeds & Nut Butter Snacks

### 21. [Almonds](/almonds) (12 almonds, ~14g) — 82 calories
A small handful of almonds provides protein, healthy fats, and fiber. Count them out rather than eating from the bag.

### 22. Pumpkin Seeds (15g) — 83 calories
A crunchy, nutrient-dense seed rich in magnesium and zinc. Great as a topping for yogurt or eaten straight.

### 23. One Rice Cake with [Peanut Butter](/peanut-butter) (1 tsp) — 68 calories
The rice cake provides crunch (35 cal) and the thin smear of peanut butter (33 cal) adds flavor and fat for satiety.

### 24. Pistachios (15g, about 20 nuts) — 86 calories
Eating pistachios from the shell slows your snacking speed and makes the experience last longer.

## Other Snacks Under 100 Calories

### 25. Plain Popcorn (3 cups air-popped) — 93 calories
One of the highest-volume snacks available. Three cups of air-popped popcorn is a generous portion that takes time to eat. Skip butter and oil to keep calories low.

### 26. Dark Chocolate (15g, ~2 small squares) — 80 calories
When a sweet craving hits, 15g of 70%+ dark chocolate satisfies it for well under 100 calories. Eat it slowly.

### 27. Miso Soup (1 cup) — 35 calories
Warm, savory, and filling for almost no calories. The umami flavor helps satisfy cravings for something rich.

### 28. Pickles (3 medium dill pickles) — 12 calories
Crunchy, salty, and tangy for essentially zero calories. Pickles are an excellent choice when you want a salty snack instead of chips.

### 29. Seaweed Snacks (1 pack, ~5g) — 25 calories
Crispy, salty, and satisfying. Pre-packaged seaweed snacks are portion-controlled by design.

### 30. Frozen Banana Bites (half a [banana](/banana), 50g, frozen) — 45 calories
Slice a banana into coins, freeze on a tray, and eat them frozen. They taste remarkably like ice cream.

## Smart Snacking Tips for Weight Loss

### Plan Your Snacks
Do not leave snacking to chance. Decide what and when you will snack at the beginning of each day and have those items ready.

### Combine Protein with Fiber or Volume
The most satisfying snacks combine a protein source with a high-volume, low-calorie food:
- [Egg](/eggs) + cherry [tomatoes](/tomatoes) = 96 calories, 7g protein
- Greek yogurt + [strawberries](/strawberries) = 90 calories, 11g protein
- [Turkey](/turkey-breast) + [cucumber](/cucumber) = 75 calories, 14g protein

### Pre-Portion Everything
Never eat directly from a large bag or container. Even healthy snacks become problematic when portion control disappears. Measure portions into small containers or bags ahead of time.

### Ask If You Are Actually Hungry
Before reaching for a snack, drink a glass of water and wait 10 minutes. Thirst and boredom account for a significant portion of unnecessary snacking.

## Key Takeaways

- Smart snacking supports weight loss by preventing extreme hunger between meals
- The best snacks under 100 calories combine protein, fiber, or high water content
- Top picks include hard-boiled [eggs](/eggs) (78 cal, 6g protein), Greek yogurt (59 cal, 10g protein), and fresh fruits like [apple](/apple) (95 cal) and [strawberries](/strawberries) (49 cal per cup)
- Air-popped popcorn (93 cal for 3 cups) and vegetables with dips offer high-volume snacking
- Pre-portion snacks and plan them in advance to avoid impulsive high-calorie choices
