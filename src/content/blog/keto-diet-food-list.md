## Introduction

The ketogenic (keto) diet is a high-fat, moderate-protein, very low-carbohydrate eating pattern that shifts your body's primary fuel source from glucose to ketones — molecules produced from the breakdown of fat. By drastically reducing carbohydrate intake to approximately 20-50 grams per day, the body enters a metabolic state called ketosis, where it becomes efficient at burning fat for energy.

Knowing exactly which foods fit within keto's strict carb limits is essential for success. This guide provides a comprehensive food list organized by category, with net carb counts for every item.

## Understanding Net Carbs

On keto, you track "net carbs" rather than total carbs:

**Net Carbs = Total Carbs - Fiber**

Fiber is subtracted because your body does not digest or absorb it for energy. For example, an [avocado](/avocado) has 9g total carbs but 7g of fiber, so its net carbs are just 2g.

## Foods to Eat on Keto

### Meat and Poultry (0g net carbs)

All unprocessed meat is naturally zero-carb and a staple of the keto diet:

- [Chicken breast](/chicken-breast) — 0g carbs, 31g protein per 100g
- Chicken thighs — 0g carbs, 26g protein per 100g
- [Beef](/beef) (any cut) — 0g carbs
- Pork (tenderloin, chops, belly) — 0g carbs
- [Lamb](/lamb) — 0g carbs
- Turkey — 0g carbs
- Bacon — 0g carbs (check for sugar-cured varieties)
- Ground beef — 0g carbs

**Watch out for:** Breaded or marinated meats, processed sausages with fillers, and deli meats with added sugars.

### Fish and Seafood (0g net carbs)

Fish is ideal for keto — zero carbs, high protein, and often rich in omega-3 fats:

- [Salmon](/salmon) — 0g carbs, 20g protein, 13g fat per 100g
- [Tuna](/tuna) — 0g carbs, 26g protein per 100g
- [Shrimp](/shrimp) — 0g carbs, 24g protein per 100g
- Sardines — 0g carbs, 25g protein per 100g
- Cod — 0g carbs, 18g protein per 100g
- Mackerel — 0g carbs, 19g protein per 100g
- Trout — 0g carbs, 20g protein per 100g

**Watch out for:** Breaded fish, imitation crab (contains starch), and fish sticks.

### Eggs (0.4g net carbs per egg)

[Eggs](/eggs) are the perfect keto food — high in fat, moderate in protein, and nearly zero carbs. Eat them any way you like: scrambled, fried, poached, hard-boiled, or in omelets.

### Healthy Fats and Oils (0g net carbs)

Fat is the primary energy source on keto. Choose quality sources:

- Olive oil — 0g carbs
- Coconut oil — 0g carbs
- Avocado oil — 0g carbs
- Butter and ghee — 0g carbs
- MCT oil — 0g carbs
- Lard and tallow — 0g carbs

## Foods to Avoid on Keto

### High-Carb Foods to Eliminate

- **Grains:** [Rice](/rice), wheat, [oats](/oats), bread, pasta, cereal, corn
- **Sugar:** Table sugar, honey, maple syrup, agave
- **Starchy vegetables:** [Potato](/potato), [sweet potato](/sweet-potato), [corn](/corn), peas
- **Most fruits:** [Banana](/banana) (23g carbs/100g), [apple](/apple) (12g), [mango](/mango) (13g), grapes (16g), pineapple (11g)
- **Legumes:** [Lentils](/lentils), chickpeas, kidney beans, black beans
- **Sugary drinks:** Soda, fruit juice, sweetened coffee, sports drinks
- **Processed foods:** Candy, cookies, cakes, chips, crackers
- **Low-fat products:** Often loaded with added sugar to compensate for taste

### Surprising Foods That Are Not Keto-Friendly

- **Milk** — 5g carbs per 100ml (lactose)
- **Balsamic vinegar** — 14g carbs per tablespoon
- **Ketchup** — 4g carbs per tablespoon
- **BBQ sauce** — 9g carbs per tablespoon
- **Honey** — 82g carbs per 100g
- **Most granola and protein bars** — 15-30g carbs per bar
- **Dried fruits** — Extremely high in concentrated sugar

## Tips for Keto Success

1. **Track net carbs carefully** for the first few weeks until you develop an intuition for carb content
2. **Eat enough fat** — fat is your primary fuel, not something to minimize
3. **Maintain adequate protein** — 1.2-1.7g per kg of body weight to preserve muscle
4. **Stay hydrated and supplement electrolytes** — keto increases water and mineral excretion (sodium, potassium, magnesium)
5. **Expect the "keto flu"** during the first week — headaches, fatigue, and irritability are normal as your body adapts
6. **Read labels carefully** — carbs hide in sauces, dressings, and processed foods

## Key Takeaways

- Keto requires limiting net carbs to 20-50g per day to achieve ketosis
- Eat freely: meat, fish, [eggs](/eggs), healthy fats, low-carb vegetables, and most cheeses
- Avoid: grains, sugar, starchy vegetables, most fruits, and legumes
- [Avocado](/avocado), leafy greens, [salmon](/salmon), [eggs](/eggs), and [almonds](/almonds) are keto staples
- Track net carbs (total carbs minus fiber) rather than total carbs
- Supplement electrolytes and drink plenty of water, especially during the first week
