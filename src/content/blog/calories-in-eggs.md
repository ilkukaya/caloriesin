## Introduction

[Eggs](/eggs) are one of the most versatile, affordable, and nutritious foods available. A single large egg packs 6 grams of complete protein, essential vitamins, and all nine essential amino acids — all for just 72 calories. But the final calorie count of your eggs depends heavily on how you cook them.

A boiled egg and a fried egg start from the same place but can end up with very different calorie counts depending on the cooking fat used. This guide breaks down the calories in eggs by every common preparation method so you can make informed choices.

## Calories in a Raw Egg

Before cooking, here is what a large egg (50g) contains:

| Nutrient | Whole Egg | Egg White Only | Egg Yolk Only |
|----------|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| Calories | 72 cal | 17 cal | 55 cal |
| Protein | 6.3g | 3.6g | 2.7g |
| Fat | 4.8g | 0.1g | 4.5g |
| Carbs | 0.4g | 0.2g | 0.6g |
| Cholesterol | 186mg | 0mg | 186mg |

The yolk contains most of the calories and fat, but also most of the nutrients — including vitamin D, vitamin B12, choline, and selenium. Whole eggs are nutritionally superior to whites alone.

## Calories by Cooking Method

### Hard-Boiled Egg — 78 calories

Hard-boiled eggs are cooked in water with no added fat, making them one of the leanest preparations. The slight increase from 72 to 78 calories reflects minor changes during cooking.

- **Calories:** 78 per large egg
- **Protein:** 6.3g
- **Fat:** 5.3g
- **Best for:** Meal prep, snacking, salads, on-the-go eating

Hard-boiled eggs keep in the refrigerator for up to one week, making them ideal for meal prep. Cook a batch on Sunday and you have protein-rich snacks and salad toppers for the entire week.

### Soft-Boiled Egg — 78 calories

Nutritionally identical to hard-boiled. The only difference is the runny yolk, which is a matter of cooking time, not calorie content.

### Poached Egg — 72 calories

Poached eggs are cooked in water without any added fat, preserving the original calorie count of the raw egg. They are one of the healthiest egg preparations.

- **Calories:** 72 per large egg
- **Protein:** 6.3g
- **Fat:** 4.8g
- **Best for:** Topping toast, salads, grain bowls, and [avocado](/avocado) toast

### Fried Egg (in oil or butter) — 90-120 calories

Frying adds calories from the cooking fat. The total depends on how much fat you use:

- Fried in cooking spray: ~78 calories (minimal added fat)
- Fried in 1 teaspoon butter: ~106 calories
- Fried in 1 teaspoon oil: ~112 calories
- Fried in 1 tablespoon butter: ~174 calories

**Tip:** Use a non-stick pan with cooking spray to get the crispy edges of a fried egg without the extra calories.

### Scrambled Eggs (2 eggs) — 180-280 calories

Scrambled eggs vary widely based on what you add:

- 2 eggs scrambled with cooking spray: ~156 calories
- 2 eggs scrambled with 1 tsp butter: ~190 calories
- 2 eggs scrambled with milk and butter: ~220 calories
- 2 eggs scrambled with cream and butter: ~280 calories

For a lower-calorie scramble, use cooking spray instead of butter and skip the milk or cream. The eggs will still be fluffy if you whisk them well and cook over low heat.

**Add vegetables for volume:** Stirring in [spinach](/spinach), [tomatoes](/tomatoes), [mushrooms](/mushrooms), and [bell peppers](/bell-peppers) adds bulk and nutrition for very few extra calories.

### Omelet (3 eggs) — 220-450+ calories

An omelet's calorie count depends entirely on its fillings:

| Omelet Type | Approximate Calories |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Plain 3-egg omelet (no oil) | 216 cal |
| Veggie omelet (spinach, mushroom, pepper) | 260 cal |
| Cheese omelet (30g cheddar) | 340 cal |
| Ham and cheese omelet | 380 cal |
| Western omelet (ham, cheese, peppers, onion) | 420 cal |
| Restaurant-style loaded omelet | 500-800 cal |

A vegetable omelet with [spinach](/spinach), [mushrooms](/mushrooms), and [tomatoes](/tomatoes) is one of the best breakfast options for weight loss — high protein, moderate calories, and very filling.

### Egg White Omelet (4 whites) — 68 calories

Using only egg whites dramatically cuts calories and fat. Four egg whites provide 14.4g of protein for just 68 calories. However, you miss the nutrients in the yolk.

**A practical compromise:** Use 2 whole eggs plus 2 egg whites. This gives you 150 calories, 18g of protein, and retains the yolk's nutrients while keeping calories moderate.

### Baked/Shirred Egg — 90 calories

Eggs baked in a ramekin with minimal fat have a similar calorie count to poached eggs, though adding cream, cheese, or butter increases calories significantly.

## How Many Eggs Can You Eat Per Day?

Current nutritional science suggests that for most healthy adults, eating 1-3 whole eggs per day is safe and beneficial. The concern about dietary cholesterol raising blood cholesterol has been largely revised — for most people, dietary cholesterol has a modest impact on blood cholesterol levels.

Eggs provide:
- **Complete protein** with all essential amino acids
- **Choline** — essential for brain health, and most people are deficient
- **Vitamin D** — one of few food sources
- **Lutein and zeaxanthin** — important for eye health
- **B vitamins** — especially B12 and riboflavin

If you have specific cardiovascular risk factors or have been advised by your doctor to limit cholesterol, follow their guidance.

## Tips for Keeping Egg Calories Low

1. **Choose boiling or poaching** over frying to avoid added fat
2. **Use non-stick pans with cooking spray** when frying or scrambling
3. **Skip the cheese** or use a small amount of strong-flavored cheese (parmesan adds flavor without many calories)
4. **Load up on vegetables** — add [spinach](/spinach), [tomatoes](/tomatoes), [mushrooms](/mushrooms), [bell peppers](/bell-peppers), and onions
5. **Mix whole eggs with egg whites** for more protein and fewer calories
6. **Avoid high-calorie toppings** like hollandaise sauce, sour cream, and excessive cheese

## Key Takeaways

- A large egg contains 72 calories, 6.3g protein, and 4.8g fat
- The lowest-calorie cooking methods are poaching (72 cal) and boiling (78 cal)
- Frying adds 18-100+ calories depending on the amount of oil or butter used
- Scrambled eggs range from 156 to 280+ calories for two eggs depending on what you add
- For weight loss, combine 2 whole eggs + 2 egg whites with vegetables for a high-protein, moderate-calorie meal
- Most healthy adults can safely eat 1-3 eggs per day
